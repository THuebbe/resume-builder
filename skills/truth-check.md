# Skill: truth-check

**Job:** Trace every claim on the resume to the fact bank. Nothing else.
**Reads:** rendered resume text, `_shared/fact-bank.md`. **Writes:** the Truth section of `04_review.md`.

## Steps
1. Split the resume into claims (each number, each "did X," each skill, each date).
2. For each, cite the fact-bank ID. Mark: Traced / Traced but stronger than the fact / Not found.
3. Any "stronger than the fact" or "Not found" blocks the stage until fixed or confirmed by Tobi.
4. Check names: employer is "Standard Bearings" only; no customer names; no Weimer.
