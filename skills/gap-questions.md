# Skill: gap-questions

**Job:** Turn Weak/Missing matches into questions only Tobi can answer. Nothing else.
**Reads:** Match table in `02_match.md`. **Writes:** the Questions section of `02_match.md`.

## Steps
1. For each Weak or Missing item that matters to the posting, write one specific question.
   Ask about experience, not about wording ("Have you ever sold into a data center or a plant
   with chillers?" not "Can we say data center experience?").
2. Offer A/B or multiple-choice framing where possible — Tobi moves faster with constrained options.
3. Order by impact on getting an interview. Cap at 6.
4. Mark which answers would change the go/no-go.

## Rules
- Never fill a gap with a guess, a plausible number, or the posting's own wording.
- Answers go into the fact bank first (`update-fact-bank.md`), then into the resume.
