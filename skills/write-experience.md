# Skill: write-experience

**Job:** Write job headers, overview sentences, bullets, highlights and project entries.
**Reads:** Selection in `02_match.md`, `_shared/fact-bank.md`, `_shared/voice.md`.
**Writes:** the Experience, Highlights and Projects blocks of `03_draft.md`.

## Job header
`**Title**, Standard Bearings, *Davenport, IA* | Month YYYY – Present`

## Overview sentence (italic, one per job)
Scope and context of the role in one sentence. Not an accomplishment.

## Bullets
- What you did → how → result. Result when the fact bank has one; never invented.
- 1–3 lines. Vary length and shape across the list.
- Start with a past or present verb that's plain ("Won," "Set up," "Replaced," "Manage").
- Customers described, never named.

## Career Highlights
`**Label:** one or two lines with a number.` Labels are plain ("Energy efficiency," "Account growth").

## Selected Projects
`**What, where.** Problem in a sentence. What Tobi did. Outcome (including honest losses).`
Plain titles ("Stainless pump base, pork processor"), never clever ones.
