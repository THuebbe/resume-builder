# Skill: go-no-go

**Job:** Decide whether the posting is worth tailoring a resume for. Nothing else.
**Reads:** Requirements from `01_intake.md`, `_shared/targets.md`, `_shared/fact-bank.md` (headline facts only).
**Writes:** the Go/No-go section of `01_intake.md`.

## Steps
1. Check the hard floor in `_shared/targets.md` (total pay, base, and the whole package). Top of the range below the floor = No-go.
2. Check location/remote and travel against `targets.md`.
3. Check knockouts against the fact bank (degree, years, licenses). Mark each Pass / Fail / Unclear.
4. Check level: overqualified (posting wants 1–3 yrs) or underqualified.
5. Check day-to-day fit against what Tobi is best at and likes less.
6. Give a call: **Go**, **Go with risks** (list them), or **No-go** (one-line reason).
7. If Go but a knockout fails, add the "reach a human" step (hiring manager / recruiter on LinkedIn).

## Rules
- Lead with the verdict in one sentence. Then the reasons.
- Don't soften a No-go. Tobi wants the weakest point first.
- Tobi makes the final call. Stage 02 doesn't start until he says go.
