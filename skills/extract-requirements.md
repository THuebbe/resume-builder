# Skill: extract-requirements

**Job:** Read a job posting and list what the employer requires, prefers and expects. Nothing else.
**Reads:** `00_posting.md`. **Writes:** the Requirements section of `01_intake.md`.
**Does not:** judge fit, pull keywords, or touch the fact bank.

## Steps
1. Read the whole posting once without writing.
2. Sort every requirement into one bucket:
   - **Must-have:** "required," "must," "minimum," years of experience, degrees, licenses.
   - **Nice-to-have:** "preferred," "a plus," "ideally," "bonus."
   - **Duties:** what the person will actually do day to day.
   - **Knockouts:** anything an ATS likely screens on: degree, years, location, travel %, clearance,
     work authorization, license.
   - **Conditions:** pay range, travel, schedule, remote/hybrid, physical demands, reporting line.
3. Quote the posting's own words for each item (short). Don't paraphrase requirements.
4. Note the level the posting implies (e.g. "1–3 years" + "support senior leaders" = junior).
5. Note anything ambiguous as a question for stage 02.

## Output format
Bulleted lists under: Must-have · Nice-to-have · Duties · Knockouts · Conditions · Level · Ambiguities.
