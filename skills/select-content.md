# Skill: select-content

**Job:** Choose what goes on this resume. Nothing else — no writing yet.
**Reads:** `02_match.md`, `_shared/fact-bank.md`, `_shared/layout.md` (page plan only).
**Writes:** the Selection section of `02_match.md`.

## Choose
1. **Title bar:** two titles. Default "Technical Sales Professional | Industrial Account Manager";
   swap the second for one that mirrors the posting's title if truthful.
2. **Areas of Expertise:** 12–16 items. Posting keywords Tobi can back up first, then his strengths.
3. **Career Highlights:** the 3 stories that best answer the posting's top needs. Pick from the
   fact bank's Stories section. No story may appear twice on the page.
4. **Current-job bullets:** 5–7, ordered by relevance to this posting.
5. **Selected Projects:** include for technical/engineering roles (2–3 projects not already used
   as highlights); drop for account-management or desk roles if page 2 still fills.
6. **Skills rows:** Products, Energy (if relevant), Tools. Reorder to lead with the posting's terms.

## Rules
- Every selected item cites its fact-bank ID.
- Prefer stories with a number in them.
