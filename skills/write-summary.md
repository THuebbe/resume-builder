# Skill: write-summary

**Job:** Write the title bar and the Professional Summary. Nothing else.
**Reads:** Selection in `02_match.md`, `_shared/fact-bank.md`, `_shared/voice.md`.
**Writes:** the Summary block of `03_draft.md`.

## Steps
1. Title bar: the two titles from Selection, separated by " | ".
2. Summary: 3 sentences, 4 max. No "I".
   - Sentence 1: who + years (10 years outside sales, 15 with Standard Bearings) + what kind of company.
   - Sentence 2: scope with numbers (180+ accounts, about $3.1M, industries, territory).
   - Sentence 3: how he wins, in the posting's language where truthful.
3. Don't repeat the title bar's exact words as the summary's first words.

## Check before handing off
- Every number matches the fact bank's safe value.
- No banned words from `voice.md`.
