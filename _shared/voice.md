# Voice — how the resume sounds

reviewed: 11-24-2026

## Do
- **Implied first person everywhere.** "Manage more than 180 accounts," not "I manage" or "He manages."
  The summary may use third-person-style fragments ("Technical sales professional with…").
- **Brag with numbers, not adjectives.** Every claim gets a number, a name of a thing, or a result.
- **Bullets tell what, how, and result** where the result exists. 1–3 lines each.
- **Vary structure.** Not every bullet the same length or shape. Not every project with a punchline.
- **Plain shop-floor words.** "Down line," "wet pit," "pulled the part from another branch."
- **Keep honest losses** when they show judgment (the wastewater bid lost on our spec).
- **Conservative numbers.** Use the safe value from the fact bank ("more than 180," "about $20,000").
- **Describe customers, never name them.** "A pork processor," "a limestone mining operation."
- **Employer = Standard Bearings.** Never Weimer, never "a Weimer company."

## Don't (AI tells recruiters screen for)
- Words: spearheaded, leveraged, orchestrated, honed, streamlined, synergy, robust, seamless, delve,
  dynamic, results-driven, passionate, detail-oriented, team player, go-getter, proven track record,
  fostering, data-driven (unless literal).
- Self-rating adjectives: seasoned, award-winning, world-class, expert (unless a credential).
- "Not just X, it's Y." Rule-of-three lists used for rhythm. Stacked hedges.
- Em dashes in resume body text.
- Slogan openers ("When the catalog doesn't have the answer, I design it.").
- "From X to Y to Z" three-step journeys.
- Clever or punny titles for projects. Use plain "What, where" titles.
- Identical bullet shapes or bold lead-ins on every bullet.
- Restating the same fact in summary, highlights and bullets with identical wording.
  (Repeating a number is fine; repeating a sentence isn't.)
- First person "I" in bullets. Exception: none on the resume. (Cover letters and LinkedIn use "I".)
- Profanity anywhere, ever.

## Words Tobi actually uses (prefer these)
- "Down line," "same day," "pulled from another branch," "plant floor," "walk the line,"
  "turnkey," "OEM and MRO," "power transmission," "fluid power," "conveying."
