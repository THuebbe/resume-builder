# 01 Intake — SEW-EURODRIVE, District Sales Specialist - Iowa

Run started 11-25-2026.

## Go/No-go

**Go with risks.** Strongest live fit found so far: manufacturer side, Iowa territory, Cedar Rapids
preferred, and the preferred degree is exactly Tobi's A.S. in Engineering. The risks are pay
structure, VFD/motion-control depth, statewide travel, and a posting that is 30+ days old.

- **Pay — Unclear.** "$110,000-$140,000 Annual Compensation" includes salary, uncapped commission
  and bonus. Floor in targets.md is $115K total with $85K+ base. The bottom of the range misses it;
  the base split is not stated. Ask the recruiter for base vs. variable and what reps typically earn.
- **Package — likely better than today.** $0 employee premium on medical/dental/vision;
  401(k) "200% Company Match on the first 3%" (6% of pay) plus profit sharing; 12 holidays; benefits
  from day one. Vehicle is not mentioned: ask (company car, allowance, or mileage).
- **Location — Pass.** "Must be located in or willing to relocate to Iowa, preferably the Des Moines
  or Cedar Rapids Areas." West Branch to Cedar Rapids is about 35 miles.
- **Travel — Risk.** "Daily travel within the sales territory and perform overnight travel as
  needed." The territory reads as all of Iowa ("covering the Iowa territory"; a third-party copy said
  the eastern two-thirds). More overnights than today. Within targets.md (regional, occasional
  overnights) unless it's frequent.
- **Knockouts:**
  - Gear reducers, electric motors — Pass (F-TECH-01, F-STORY-02/03/12).
  - Variable frequency drives, motion controllers — Unclear. The posting says "experience with
    and/or knowledge of," so knowledge counts. Fact bank has VFD training only as [confirm]
    (F-EDU-05) and PLC basics (F-TECH-01). Question for Tobi below.
  - Associate degree in engineering — Pass, preferred and exact (F-EDU-01).
  - Two years outside sales — Pass, ten years (F-JOB-01).
  - Driver's license, Iowa residency, work authorization — Pass.
- **Level — Right step.** Individual contributor on SEW's "Midwest Sales Team," with "clear career
  growth opportunities." A manufacturer territory is the first rung toward regional sales manager.
  Not overqualified at the pay quoted.
- **Day-to-day — Strong fit.** Prospecting and converting leads (F-SALES-15: opened most of his
  active book), technical solutions, customer training, and selling through distributors, OEMs and
  end users. Ten years inside a distributor is an edge: he knows how SEW's distributors buy.
- **Timing — Risk.** "Posted 30+ Days Ago." Apply fast, then reach a human: find SEW's Midwest
  regional sales manager on LinkedIn and send a short note after applying.
- **Possible conflict.** If Standard Bearings is an SEW distributor, Tobi would sell to his former
  branch. Common in this industry; not a knockout. Check non-compete or non-solicit terms first.

**Level**

  Tobi's; the top of the pay range is reserved for "extensive experience."

**Ambiguities (for stage 02 or the recruiter)**
